Download "mysql-connector-j-<version>.jar" (MySQL Connector/J) and place the .jar
file directly in this WEB-INF/lib folder. In Eclipse, right click the project ->
Refresh, then it will automatically be added to the deployment assembly / build path.

Recommended: mysql-connector-j-8.4.0.jar or newer, from:
https://dev.mysql.com/downloads/connector/j/
